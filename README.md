## Professional Data Analytics projects

This repository showcases selected data analytics projects completed during my professional experience, demonstrating practical application of data management, analysis, reporting, and visualization in a real-world public health environment.


> **Note:** Due to departmental data-sharing and confidentiality restrictions, original reports and datasets for selected projects are not included. The project descriptions highlight the analytical scope, tools used, and key contributions.

### PROJECT 1: COVID-19 Data Management & Reporting
- Worked on daily data management, validation, analysis, and reporting of COVID-19 cases. The resulting reports were published publicly on the organization's website on a daily basis in PDF format.
- Reporting areas included:
    * COVID-19 testing — daily and cumulative tests conducted
    * Positive cases — overall and ward-wise counts
    * Positivity rate — overall, ward-wise, gender-wise, and age-group-wise
    * Containment zones — daily counts
    * Hospital capacity — bed capacity and availability
    * Contact tracing and quarantine
    * Vaccination — category-wise daily and cumulative reporting
    * Laboratory TAT (Turnaround Time) monitoring
    * Oxygen capacity and availability
    * Vaccine stock monitoring
    * Antigen kit stock monitoring based on previous consumption patterns

  #### Tools & Technologies:
  Excel | Power BI | SQL | Google Sheets

### PROJECT 2: Routine Immunization Data Management & Reporting
- Managed and analyzed routine immunization data to track coverage rates, missed doses, and vaccine stock levels.
- Identified areas with low vaccination coverage, helping to optimize outreach and intervention programs.
- Worked closely with health departments and stakeholders to streamline data collection, validation, and reporting.
 #### Tools & Technologies:
  Excel | Google Sheets 

### PROJECT 3: Measles Outbreak Reporting
- Monitored measles case trends to identify spikes and declines, and prepared reports with clear data visualizations for public health professionals
- Collaborated with health agencies and stakeholders to ensure accurate reporting and timely decision-making.
#### Tools & Technologies:
  Excel | Power BI | Google Sheets
